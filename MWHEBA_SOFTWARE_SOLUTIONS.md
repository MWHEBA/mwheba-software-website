## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Positioning

**MWHEBA Software Solutions** is the specialized enterprise software engineering entity within the **MWHEBA Ecosystem**.

> **"Software Built Around Your Business."**  
> *(Business First. Technology Second.)*

MWHEBA analyzes real operational workflows, eliminates systemic friction, and engineers bespoke technology around how a company actually operates, releasing businesses from the constraints of rigid off-the-shelf software and the frustrating limitation: *"Unfortunately, this option is not available."*

## 6. Technology Philosophy & Proven Stack

MWHEBA applies a **pragmatic, problem-first engineering philosophy**:
> *"Technology is chosen strictly for the operational problem — not for industry hype."*

### Preferred & Proven Technologies:
* **Backend & Core Logic:** Python (`Django`, `FastAPI`), with modular architecture adaptable to other proven enterprise runtimes when project specifications demand.
* **Frontend Dashboards & Portals:** `React.js` (for interactive dashboards), `Alpine.js` & `HTMX` (for lightweight high-speed responsive views).
* **UI Styling & Design Systems:** `Tailwind CSS`, `Bootstrap`, and clean CSS Custom Properties (`:root` tokens) adhering to flat-color corporate minimalism.
* **Database & Persistence:** `MySQL` / `PostgreSQL` with strict transactional isolation, ACID compliance, and foreign key integrity.
* **Security & Reliability (Priority #1):** Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), end-to-end TLS/HTTPS, automated transactional audit logs (`Audit Trail`), and scheduled disaster recovery.

## 7. The 7-Stage Predictable Engineering Timeline

```
01. DISCOVER  ──► 02. ARCHITECT ──► 03. UI/UX ──► 04. DEVELOP ──► 05. TEST ──► 06. DEPLOY ──► 07. SLA SUPPORT
  Audit Flow       Data Model       Screens        Core Build       QA / Stress     Rollout        Post-Launch
```

1. **01. Discovery & Workflow Audit:** Deep dive into the client's current operational reality, paper forms, spreadsheets, and departmental friction points.
2. **02. System Architecture & Data Modeling:** Structuring database schemas, operational modules, access tiers, and API pipelines.
3. **03. UI/UX & Prototyping:** Designing clean, intuitive, low-friction interfaces tailored for rapid daily business input.
4. **04. Modular Core Development:** Coding modular, extensible business components with reusable enterprise architecture.
5. **05. QA & Stress Testing:** Rigorous financial calculation validation, reconciliation testing, transactional data-integrity testing, and security vulnerability assessments.
6. **06. Staging, Deployment & Team Training:** Data migration from legacy files, production setup, and hands-on staff onboarding.
7. **07. Post-Launch Support & SLA:** Support coverage, warranty period, and SLA terms are defined according to the project agreement (with optional renewable maintenance plans).

## 8. Intellectual Property & Licensing Clarity

MWHEBA enforces a clear, professional, and transparent IP governance model:

* **Client Ownership:** The client holds **100% unconditional ownership** of their business data, uploaded assets, customer databases, and proprietary commercial content.
* **Software Licensing Model:** For bespoke custom software and ERP deployments, the primary licensing model is a **Perpetual Operational License (رخصة تشغيل دائمة)** granting permanent, unhindered operational rights without artificial seat taxes.
* **MWHEBA Proprietary Assets:** Core frameworks, reusable base modules, shared utility libraries, and proprietary software architecture remain the intellectual property of MWHEBA.
* **Project-Specific Custom Code:** Handover and custom source code terms are clearly specified contractually per project scope and licensing tier.

## 10. Multi-Step Lead Qualification Workflow

All inbound project inquiries are systematically qualified through a 3-step structured intake:

1. **Step 1 — Business Domain:** Selection of industry type (Services, Retail/Wholesale, Academy, School, Manufacturing, Medical, Custom).
2. **Step 2 — Operational Challenge:** Identification of primary bottleneck (Branch Sync, Custom ERP, Inventory/Invoicing, User Portals, Full Automation).
3. **Step 3 — Contact & Verification:** Company name, decision-maker contact (WhatsApp/Phone), expected team scale, and consultation scheduling.
4. **Lead Closing Handoff:** Pre-populated direct WhatsApp message linking the prospect to a systems architect with their unique Reference Code (`MW-XXXXXX`).
